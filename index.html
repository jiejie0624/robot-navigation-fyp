Compare planners and export data

Both planners often choose the same route because they optimize the same shortest-path cost. Their difference is search effort after map updates: A* starts over, while D* Lite reuses its previous search state.

End-to-end comparison uses 6 fixed scenarios and 18 repeatable random maps (6 seeds at each of 8%, 16%, and 24% obstacle density), with 10 runs per planner and map. The controlled comparison gives both planners the same map updates and positions. Sensor range: 3 cells. The physical robot stage can later replace the simulated sensor with a real range sensor.
